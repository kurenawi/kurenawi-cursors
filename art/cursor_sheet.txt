PIXEL STYLE
Crisp 16-bit game pixel art with clearly visible square pixels, 1px dark outline, restrained palette (black, crimson, warm skin, white highlights), gentle cel shading. No blur, no anti-aliasing, no gradients.

SHEET LAYOUT
ONE still square image: exactly 4 columns x 4 rows = 16 equal square cells. Same character, same size, same palette and same design in every cell; only the expression, hands and props change.
Each figure is centered in its cell and fills about 75% of the cell; nothing touches or crosses cell borders. One figure per cell.
No grid lines, borders, numbers, text, watermark, cursor arrows or icons, floor, shadow or scenery.
